# 100 ChatGPT Prompts for Writing Viral Content

This curated collection of 100 ChatGPT prompts is organized by content category for rapid deployment. Each prompt is designed to generate production-quality output with minimal iteration.

## Social Media & Marketing

1. "Write a Twitter thread explaining [topic] in 10 tweets with a hook, insight drops, and a conversion CTA."
2. "Generate 5 LinkedIn post variants positioning me as a thought leader on [subject]."
3. "Create 20 trending hashtags for [niche] optimized for discoverability."

## SEO & Blog Content

4. "Write a 1500-word SEO-optimized blog post about [keyword] targeting [audience]. Include H2/H3 structure, meta description, and 5 internal linking opportunities."
5. "Generate 10 FAQ schema entries for [topic] with answers optimized for featured snippets."

## Technical Writing

6. "Explain [technology] at three levels: beginner (analogy), intermediate (architecture), advanced (implementation)."
7. "Write a README for [project] following standard open-source conventions."
