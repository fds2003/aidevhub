# 45 DALL-E 3 Use Cases with Proven Prompts

## Product Photography

Generate professional product shots without a studio. Prompt pattern: "Professional product photography of [item] on [background], studio lighting, shallow depth of field, 8K."

## Marketing Assets

Create campaign visuals with consistent branding. "Social media banner for [campaign], [brand colors], modern design, text placeholder [headline], 1200x628px."

## UI/UX Mockups

Rapidly prototype interface concepts. "Mobile app screen for [feature], iOS design system, minimalist, clean, high-fidelity mockup."

## Technical Diagrams

Visualize architectures and workflows. "System architecture diagram showing [components], connected by arrows, labeled, technical blueprint style."
